About the Portal

Occidental Mindoro National High School Portal

This is a student-created website demonstration intended to show how a school portal might organize online enrollment, student access, document requests, announcements, and other student services.

It is not an official website or service of Occidental Mindoro National High School or the Department of Education.

Privacy reminder: Do not submit actual student names, addresses, birth dates, contact details, school documents, or other personal information. Use sample data only.
