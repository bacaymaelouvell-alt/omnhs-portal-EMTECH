ABOUT THE PROJECT

About OMNHS Lost and Found

A school community platform designed to make reporting and returning lost belongings easier.

Our Purpose

The OMNHS Lost and Found Portal provides a convenient way for students and members of the school community to report missing belongings and submit found-item reports. It also helps connect possible owners with their belongings through an administrator-reviewed process.
